Turn your study sets into an asteroid-defending typing game.

Type the correct answer before an asteroid reaches your planet. Practise vocabulary, facts and more with your own terms and definitions.

FIND OR CREATE YOUR NEXT SET
Browse and search shared sets, import a public Quizlet set, write your own cards or upload a CSV. Share a set link so friends can study and compete on the same leaderboard.

PLAY YOUR WAY
Choose from five difficulty modes. Answer with terms, definitions or both, and focus on starred cards when you want extra practice.

LEARN FROM MISSED ANSWERS
Miss an asteroid and practise typing the correct answer. Watch out for red asteroids: missing the same term twice ends the game.

CHALLENGE YOUR PERSONAL BEST
Build a streak and earn points. Shared-set leaderboards keep your highest score for each set. Play with a guest username or create an account.

ON iPHONE, iPAD AND MAC
Play with touch controls or a keyboard. An internet connection is required.
