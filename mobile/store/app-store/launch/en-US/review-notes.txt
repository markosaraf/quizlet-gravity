Quizlet Gravity is a flashcard-based typing game for iPhone, iPad and Mac. It requires an internet connection and loads the production service at https://www.quizletgravity.com/.

To review without registering:
1. Select Try Example Game on the landing page. It starts immediately and requires no account.
2. Type answers to the arithmetic questions on the asteroids. Exit returns to the games page.
3. Alternatively choose a shared game, create your own game, or import a public Quizlet set.
4. Missing a term opens the correct-answer step. Missing a red asteroid ends the game.

For shared-set leaderboards, choose a guest username, open a shared set and select Play this game. Account registration uses email verification. Google and Sign in with Apple are also offered. Account deletion is available from Your account → Delete account. Deleted accounts retain anonymised leaderboard scores as Deleted user. No purchases or subscriptions are sold in the app.

The Mac app supports Intel and Apple silicon. It uses a desktop layout and physical keyboard input.

Review contact: quizletgravity@gmail.com

User safety: shared sets and leaderboard entries offer reporting and personal blocking. Blocked users can be managed from Your account. Administrators review reports in the moderation queue.
