Learn by playing games. Challenge friends on the leaderboard.

Turn flashcards into a space game: type the correct answers before the asteroids reach your planet.

FIND A GAME
Try the example game with one tap, or search and choose a shared game by title or creator.

CREATE & IMPORT
Create your own game with terms, definitions and images. Copy and paste a public Quizlet set link to add and play it, or import and edit it to make your own version.

PLAY & LEARN
Choose your difficulty and practise answering with terms, definitions or both. When you miss an answer, practise the correction before continuing. Watch out for red asteroids: missing one ends the game.

CHALLENGE FRIENDS
Share a game link and compete on its leaderboard. Play with a guest username or sign in to an account. Only your highest score for each game appears on its leaderboard.

An internet connection is required. Quizlet imports depend on the original set being public and accessible.
